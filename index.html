How I work

Sized to your business. Not someone else's.

Three principles that have kept clients with me for decades. They're not slogans — they're how the work actually gets done.

No surprise contracts.

I bill at an hourly rate for the work you actually need. Monthly support arrangements exist when they make sense for your business — and not because a contract demands it.

No oversized stack.

I bring tools in when they earn their place. A 12-person firm doesn't need the same systems a 1,200-person firm runs on, and I won't pretend otherwise.
